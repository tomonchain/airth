At least once every 12 months, the company confirms the information Companies House holds about it. The review period ends 12 months after the date of incorporation for the first statement, then 12 months after the confirmation date on the last statement filed. The statement is due within 14 days after the review period ends.

Filing early moves the next review period: it then counts from the confirmation date the company chose, which the founder enters as `confirmation_date`.

Checked against Companies House's record for Hedgen Ltd: incorporated 7 November 2025, first statement date 6 November 2026, due by 20 November 2026.
