The Company Tax Return (CT600) is due 12 months after the end of the accounting period it covers. When the period of accounts is longer than 12 months, it holds two Corporation Tax periods and so two returns, both due 12 months after the end of the period of accounts (GOV.UK's example: a year extended to 31 March 2025, all returns filed by 31 March 2026).

A late return is penalised even when there is no tax to pay.
