For taxable profits up to £1.5 million, Corporation Tax is due 9 months and 1 day after the end of each Corporation Tax period. A Corporation Tax period is at most 12 months: a first period of accounts longer than that splits in two, the first 12 months from incorporation, then the rest (GOV.UK's example: set up 11 May 2024, accounts to 31 May 2025, periods 11 May 2024 to 10 May 2025 and 11 May 2025 to 31 May 2025). HMRC's letter on the first periods is the authority.

Months count from the corresponding date. From a month's last day, sources differ (30 November gives 31 August or 1 September): Airth takes the earlier. Profits over £1.5 million are paid in instalments, which this rule does not cover.
