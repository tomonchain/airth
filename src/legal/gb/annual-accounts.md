A private company files its accounts within 9 months after the end of its accounting reference period (s442(2)). When the first period is longer than 12 months, the deadline is the later of 9 months after the first anniversary of incorporation (21 months after incorporation) and 3 months after the end of the period (s442(3)).

The first accounting reference period runs from incorporation to the accounting reference date, more than 6 and at most 18 months later (s391(5)).

Months count as the Companies Act says (s443): from the last day of a month, the period ends on the last day of the due month.

Checked against Companies House's record for a company incorporated on 7 November 2025: first accounts made up to 30 November 2026, due by 7 August 2027.
