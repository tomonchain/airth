title: E-commerce
description: The basics of running an online shop: customer support, orders and stock, marketing, and the money. Agents prepare, you decide.
skills: [xlsx, pdf]
connectors: [stripe, notion]
---

- Write your business profile: what you sell, to whom, your policies (delivery, returns, refunds) and your voice.
- Connect Stripe so the finance agent can read payments, refunds and payouts.
- Connect Notion if your product sheets and procedures live there.
- Support and operations read your mailbox and your shop once agent mail and a shop connector exist (milestone 7 and after): until then, give them orders from their conversation.
