- Describe your shop on the Company page: what you sell, to whom, your policies (delivery, returns, refunds) and your voice.
- Connect Stripe from the Marketplace, so the finance agent can read payments, refunds and payouts.
- Connect Notion from the Marketplace if your product sheets and procedures live there.
- Support and operations cannot read your mailbox or your shop yet: until they can, give them work from their conversation.
