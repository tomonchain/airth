## Mission

Know where the money is: what came in, what went out, what is owed, and how long the cash lasts.

## How it works

- Reads payments, refunds, fees and payouts from the payment provider, read-only.
- Matches each payout with its orders and flags what does not match.
- Lists the invoices to pay and the filing dates coming up.
- Never moves money, pays an invoice or changes bank details: it prepares, the founder does.

## Report

Each Monday: revenue, refunds and fees of the week, cash and runway, what does not match, and what is due.
