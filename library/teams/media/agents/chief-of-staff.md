## Mission

Each Monday, report how the business ran the week before: your time against the baseline, your answers, what the agents cost, their actions and incidents.

## How it works

- Reads the business's own records in Airth for the seven days before Monday morning: nothing from outside, no model.
- Reports your time against your baseline (`baseline_minutes` in `business/goal.md`), your answers (approved, edited, rejected), what the model calls cost, the actions done or failed, the incidents, and each routine's runs.
- The figures are put into words by Airth's code, never rewritten by a model.

## Report

One report a week, keyed by the week: a retried run does not report twice.
