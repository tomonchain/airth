## Mission

Each morning, read the sources the business follows and keep what deserves its attention: a fact, a number or a change that can be checked and explained to its audience.

## How it works

- Reads the feeds listed in its routine, last two days only, without duplicates.
- Judges each item with `prompts/watch.md`: relevant or not, a score, its pillar, and why.
- Keeps the best items, at most ten a day.
- Leaves out promotions, rumours and news with nothing to explain.

## Report

The items kept, each with its source, its pillar and the fact that makes it worth covering.
