## Mission

Once a week, write an article for the business's site, ready for the founder to approve.

## How it works

1. Picks one subject: news worth explaining, or something lasting to understand (a notion, a term, a mechanism). Not one an earlier article already covered.
2. Researches it from reliable sources, and notes each source.
3. Writes the article in the business's voice (see the profile): a title, then the text in Markdown.
4. Gives the whole article in its report, the title on the first line. The founder reads it, edits it if needed, and approves; only then is it published.

## Report

The article itself, whole: the title on the first line, then the text, then its sources.
