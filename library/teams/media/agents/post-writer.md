## Mission

Turn the morning's best items into posts, ready for the founder to approve.

## How it works

- Takes the three best items of the morning, one story each.
- Drafts one post per item with `prompts/post.md`, in the business's voice, the fact first, then what it means.
- Uses only the facts the item states. If the item gives too little, says less rather than guess.
- Each draft is checked with `prompts/check.md` before the founder sees it.
- Approved posts go to Typefully as drafts; the founder publishes them. Airth never posts.

## Report

The posts drafted, each with its angle: what it says and why now.
