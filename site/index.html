Your online company HQ.

AI staff, you decide.

Airth gives each of your online businesses a staff of AI agents, organised by department. They do the work. You set the goal and decide whatever commits the business.

How it works

Run a company the way a founder should.

Each business in Airth is a company: its own folder, data, secrets and team. The HQ shows you all of them at once, and what each one needs from you.

Describe the business

What it does, for whom, how it sounds, and where it is going. The agents work from what you write, in plain files you can edit.

Hire a team

Install a ready team from the Marketplace, Media or E-commerce, or hire agents one role at a time. Each agent has a mission, routines and its own rights.

Decide what matters

Drafts, replies and anything that commits the business wait in Needs you, on the web or on your phone through Telegram. Approve, edit or reject.

Businesses kept apart

No agent works for two businesses, and no data crosses between them. Each one has its own database, out of the agents' reach.

Routines you can read

Recurring work is a graph of blocks you see and edit: read a source, ask the AI, wait for you, act. The model never holds the tools that act.

Missions on demand

Ask the team for anything from the command bar. The right agent takes it, works in its sandbox, and brings back the result for review.

Budgets that stop the work

A monthly budget per business and per agent. Every model call is recorded with its cost, and the work stops when the budget is reached.

Your keys, your models

Claude, GPT, Grok, DeepSeek, Mistral, OpenRouter, or a local model with Ollama. Credentials stay with Airth and never reach the model.

Your time, measured

Airth records the time you spend on each business and the cost of its agents, so you can see whether the team really saves you work.

You decide

Agents earn their freedom, one action at a time.

Every action an agent can take has a level. Reads and reversible changes start free; anything that commits the business starts at Propose. Only you raise a level, and an incident lowers it on its own.

0PrepareThe agent prepares the action. You carry it out.
1ProposeIt waits in Needs you. You approve, edit or reject.
2Act with delayThe agent acts after a window in which you can cancel.
3ActThe agent acts, then reports.

Payments, refunds, contracts, merging code and deploying to production never go past Propose. You always have the last word on them.

Install

Start in a few minutes.

You need a Mac and a key from a model provider, Anthropic or another. A guide sets up your first business, its first agent and its first routine.

Download the app

Open the disk image and drag Airth to Applications. It then offers each new version on its own.

The app is not signed by Apple yet: the first time, open it with right-click, then Open.

Download for Mac

Or build it from source

With Rust installed:

git clone https://github.com/tomonchain/airth
cd airth
cargo install --path .
airth secret set ANTHROPIC_API_KEY --all-realms
airth service install   # starts at login, adds Airth.app
